About This Journey

Where Vietnam Reveals Its Deepest Soul

The north is where Vietnam feels most itself — ancient, layered, and unhurried in ways the south no longer is. This journey is for travelers who want more than a checklist. Who want to understand a place, not just visit it.

You'll begin in Hanoi, a city that has resisted modernity with quiet dignity. Two full days to let its rhythms settle — the honk of motorbikes around Hoan Kiem Lake at 6am, the steam rising from a bowl of phở in a doorway that's served the same family recipe since 1952. Then north by overnight sleeper train to Sapa, where the mountains open and the terraced rice fields look drawn by hand.

The journey ends on Ha Long Bay, aboard a traditional wooden junk. Three nights surrounded by 1,600 limestone karsts rising from jade-green water. Kayaking through caves. Watching fishermen cast nets at dawn. Eating dinner on deck as the mist comes in. This is the Vietnam people talk about for the rest of their lives.

Day by Day

Your 10-Day Itinerary

Click any day to expand. All times adjust to your group's pace.

1

Arrive Hanoi — Welcome & First Impressions

Airport transfer · Old Quarter walk · Welcome dinner

Private airport transfer to your boutique hotel in the French Quarter — a converted 1920s colonial villa two blocks from Hoan Kiem Lake. Check in, freshen up.
Late afternoon free time. No agenda. Let the city come to you — the motorbike noise, the incense, the street vendors. Walk the lake. Sit at a café. Arrive slowly.
Evening: welcome dinner at a rooftop restaurant with your group and guide Minh. Northern cuisine, bia hơi, and the beginning of something.
🍽 Welcome Dinner🏨 La Siesta Premium, Hanoi🟢 Easy Day
2

Hanoi Deep Dive

Old Quarter · Temple of Literature · Street food evening

Guided walk through the 36 ancient trades of the Old Quarter. Each street named for what was sold here 700 years ago — Tin Street, Paper Street, Silver Street.
Temple of Literature (Vietnam's first university, 1076 AD), Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum district, cyclo through the French Quarter.
Evening: street food tour — bún chả, bánh mì, egg coffee, and Minh's personal shortlist of the city's best hidden corners.
🍽 Breakfast + Street Food🏨 La Siesta Premium, Hanoi🟢 Easy / Moderate
3

Hanoi → Sapa by Overnight Train

Free morning · Evening departure · Private sleeper cabin

Morning at leisure — the weekend market at Dong Xuan, or simply wander. A gentle Hanoi morning before the mountains.
9pm: overnight sleeper train from Hanoi to Lao Cai (gateway to Sapa). Private 4-berth teak-panelled cabin. Arrive 6am.
Bring something warm — the mountains get cold overnight. And earplugs, though the rhythm of the train is its own kind of lullaby.
🍽 Breakfast🚂 Overnight Sleeper Train🟢 Easy
4

Sapa — Cat Cat Village Trek

Black Hmong village · Rice terrace walks · Local market

Arrive Lao Cai at 6am, transfer to Sapa (45 min). Check in, freshen up — then out immediately. The best light is morning.
Trek from Sapa town through Cat Cat village, home of the Black Hmong. Descends through terraced fields and bamboo groves. ~4 hours walking, mostly downhill.
Lunch at a family restaurant with views across the valley. Afternoon free in Sapa town — browse the Saturday handicraft market if timing aligns.
🍽 Breakfast + Lunch🏨 Pao's Sapa Leisure Hotel🟡 Moderate (4–5hr)
5

Sapa — Ta Van Valley & Family Dinner

Deeper valley trek · Dao village · Home-cooked dinner

The best day in Sapa. Ta Van valley — deeper, quieter, and more spectacularly terraced than the Cat Cat route. Red Dao villages along the way. ~5 hours.
Optional: Fansipan cable car (Southeast Asia's highest peak, 3,143m). Views across into China and Laos on a clear day. Not included but easily arranged.
Evening: the moment past travelers mention most. A Dao family invites us into their home. Twelve people at a low table, fragrant rice wine, dishes you couldn't order anywhere. Minh translates. Something shifts.
🍽 All Meals Included🏨 Pao's Sapa Leisure Hotel🟡 Moderate (5–6hr)
6

Sapa → Ha Long Bay — Board the Junk

Transfer · Check-in · Sunset cruise · First night at sea

Morning transfer Sapa → Lao Cai, then private van to Ha Long City (~5 hours with scenic lunch stop).
Afternoon: board our traditional wooden junk at Ha Long harbour. Your private cabin — teak-panelled, bay-facing porthole — awaits.
Sunset cruise deeper into the bay. Dinner on deck as the stars emerge. The bay at night sounds like nothing else on earth.
🍽 Breakfast + Dinner⛵ Orchid Classic Cruise🟢 Easy
7

Ha Long — Kayaking, Caves & Floating Village

Kayak · Surprise Cave · Cua Van floating village · Cooking class

Morning: kayaking through hidden lagoons accessible only by paddling through low cave arches. The feeling of discovering something untouched.
Explore Surprise Cave (Hang Sung Sot) — the largest grotto in the bay, with chambers large enough to house cathedrals.
Afternoon: visit Cua Van floating fishing village. Four generations of families who have lived on the water.
Evening: on-deck cooking class with the ship's chef. Learn to make bánh cuốn (steamed rice rolls). Dinner under lanterns.
🍽 All Meals⛵ Orchid Classic Cruise🟡 Moderate
8

Ha Long — Sunrise Tai Chi & Free Waters

Sunrise · Swimming · Rock climbing (optional) · Farewell dinner

5:45am: optional sunrise tai chi on the sun deck. The bay is mirror-still at this hour. The karsts appear and disappear in the mist.
Morning: swimming off the junk in a sheltered cove. Optional rock climbing on karst limestone, equipment provided.
Afternoon anchored in a secluded bay. Reading, napping, watching the water change colour as the sun moves. This is what you came for.
Evening: farewell dinner on deck. The chef's signature seafood feast. A sunset you'll photograph badly and remember perfectly.
🍽 All Meals + Farewell Dinner⛵ Orchid Classic Cruise🟢 Easy / Your Pace
9

Ha Long → Hanoi — Free Afternoon

Disembark · Return to Hanoi · Hanoi at leisure

Final breakfast on the bay. Disembark and transfer back to Hanoi (3.5 hours). Arrive early afternoon.
Afternoon entirely free — revisit a favourite street, return to a restaurant that moved you, or sit at a café by Hoan Kiem Lake and watch the city.
Optional: day trip to Ninh Binh — "Ha Long Bay on land" — dramatic karst scenery explored by sampan boat. Arrange independently or with Minh's guidance.
🍽 Breakfast🏨 La Siesta Premium, Hanoi🟢 Easy
10

Departure — Until We Meet Again

Breakfast · Airport transfer · Tour ends

Private transfer to Noi Bai International Airport at your requested time. Breakfast included at the hotel before departure.
The HiddenPath WhatsApp channel stays active — Minh will happily answer questions from the airport lounge or the plane home.

Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

What fitness level do I need?

You don’t need to be an athlete, but you should be comfortable walking 5–6 hours on the Sapa trekking days (Days 4 and 5). Terrain includes uneven paths and moderate inclines. All other days are entirely relaxed. We recommend light exercise in the weeks before departure — not for the hard moments, but so you enjoy the beautiful ones more.

What’s the best time of year?

September–November is our favourite: harvest season in Sapa (the terraces are golden), cooler Hanoi, and calmer Ha Long seas. March–April is also beautiful — drier and perfectly mild. We avoid July–August due to typhoon season and peak domestic crowds.

Is there a solo traveler supplement?

Yes — the published price assumes double occupancy. Solo travelers pay a supplement of $320. Alternatively we can try to match you with another solo traveler from the group — done on request, subject to availability. Ask us on the free call.

Can I extend or customise the itinerary?

Absolutely — this is one of our favourite conversations. Common extensions: 3 days in Ninh Binh before the tour, or continuing south to Hội An after. We can also upgrade individual elements: premium junk cabin, private driver, extended cooking class. Bring it up on the call.

What is your cancellation policy?

20% deposit to confirm, balance due 60 days before departure. Cancellations 60+ days out: full deposit refund. 60–30 days: 50% refund. Within 30 days: non-refundable — this is why we strongly recommend travel insurance. If we ever cancel, full refunds are issued immediately.

Do I need a Vietnam visa?

Most Western nationalities (UK, EU, USA, Australia, Canada) receive a 90-day e-visa on arrival — apply at evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn about 3–4 weeks before. Cost approx $25 USD. We send a step-by-step guide once you’ve confirmed your spot.
